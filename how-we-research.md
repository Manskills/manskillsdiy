**Every guide on Manskills DIY follows the same rules:**

1. **Specs come from a source, or they're flagged.** Fluid capacities, torque values, part
   numbers, and sizes need a cited source. We prefer the owner's manual, a factory service
   manual, or an OEM parts catalog. We list those sources at the bottom of the guide. If we haven't
   confirmed a number, the guide says **"Not yet verified."** We don't present a guess as fact.
2. **Specs vary by model and engine.** When a vehicle or appliance came in several versions,
   we say which version a number applies to.
3. **Safety is part of the instructions.** Jack stands, shutoff valves, eye protection, and
   electrical safety are covered before the steps, not after.
4. **Paid links are labeled.** We may earn a commission on some links, and those links are
   marked. Commissions never decide what we recommend. See the
   [affiliate disclosure]({{ '/affiliate-disclosure/' | relative_url }}).
5. **Guides get updated.** Each guide shows its last-updated date. Contact info for
   reporting errors is coming soon.
