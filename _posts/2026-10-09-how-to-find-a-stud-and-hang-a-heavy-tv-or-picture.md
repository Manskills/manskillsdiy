**Nobody taught me this.** A heavy mirror or TV hung on plain drywall will eventually come down.
The fix is finding the studs, the wood framing behind the wall, and fastening into them.

## Step-by-step

### Find the stud

1. **Plan the spot.** Decide on height and center, and mark it lightly with painter's tape.
2. **Calibrate the stud finder<!-- AFF:amazon:stud-finder -->**
   by placing it flat on the wall where there's no stud and turning it on (follow its instructions).

   ![Handheld electronic stud finder / wall scanner]({{ '/assets/images/how-to-find-a-stud-and-hang-a-heavy-tv-or-picture/find-step02-electronic-stud-finder.jpg' | relative_url }}){: loading="lazy"}
   <small class="photo-credit">Photo: [-stk](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bosch_GMS_120_P1110437.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), via Wikimedia Commons; resized.</small>

3. **Slide it slowly sideways** across the wall. Mark where it says a stud starts, then come from the
   other direction and mark where it ends. The center is halfway between. {% comment %}SHOT LIST (original photo needed): Stud finder on the wall with two pencil/painter's-tape marks showing where the stud edges are, center marked between them.{% endcomment %}
4. **Confirm it's a stud.** Check above and below on the same line (studs run floor to ceiling), and
   measure 16 inches to each side; you should find neighbors there (or at 24).
5. **Double-check with a tiny pilot hole** right where the fastener will go. You should feel wood
   resistance after the drywall. If the bit pops through into nothing, you missed. {% comment %}SHOT LIST (original photo needed): Drill with a 1/16 in bit entering the wall right at the center mark (inside the area the plate/picture will cover).{% endcomment %}

### Hanging a heavy picture or mirror

1. **Put at least one fastener into a stud.** Use a screw or picture hook rated for the weight.
2. **If you can't hit a stud,** use a heavy-duty anchor<!-- AFF:amazon:heavy-duty-drywall-anchors --> rated well above the item's weight, following
   the package instructions.
3. **Level it** and step back before you call it done.

   ![A level on top of a cabinet being installed. Use it the same way on the top edge of a frame]({{ '/assets/images/how-to-find-a-stud-and-hang-a-heavy-tv-or-picture/picture-step03-using-a-level.jpg' | relative_url }}){: loading="lazy"}
   <small class="photo-credit">Photo: [Tomwsulcer](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kitchen_renovation_9a_installing_cabinet_to_hold_microwave_using_level_and_boards.JPG), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.</small>

### Mounting a TV

1. **Check the mount.** It must be rated for your TV's weight, size, and VESA hole pattern. Read the
   mount's instructions all the way through first.
2. **Hold the wall plate up with a helper,** spanning at least two studs, and level it. Never let one
   side of the plate rely on drywall anchors. {% comment %}SHOT LIST (original photo needed): Two people holding a TV wall plate on the wall with a level on top, plate slots lined up over two marked studs.{% endcomment %}
3. **Mark the bolt holes** through the plate onto the stud centers (at least two studs). If you're
   using a full-motion arm, be extra careful here: an extended arm adds leverage on the bolts.
4. **Drill pilot holes** using the bit size the mount's instructions specify.
5. **Drive the lag bolts** with washers through the plate into the studs with a socket wrench. Snug
    them firmly; don't strip them. {% comment %}SHOT LIST (original photo needed): Socket wrench driving a lag bolt with washer through the wall plate into the stud center.{% endcomment %}

   ![Two hex-head lag screws (lag bolts) shown next to a pen and toothbrush for scale]({{ '/assets/images/how-to-find-a-stud-and-hang-a-heavy-tv-or-picture/tv-step05-lag-screws.jpg' | relative_url }}){: loading="lazy"}
   <small class="photo-credit">Photo: [Three-quarter-ten](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lag_screws_aka_lag_bolts_002.png), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.</small>

6. **Attach the brackets to the TV,** then lift the TV onto the wall plate with your helper and lock
    it in per the instructions.
7. **Tug test.** Gently pull the bottom of the TV to make sure it's locked and solid. {% comment %}SHOT LIST (original photo needed): TV mounted, hand gently pulling the bottom edge to check the lock.{% endcomment %}

## Common mistakes

- Trusting a single stud finder beep without confirming
- Drilling straight above or below an outlet where wires run
- Hanging a TV on drywall anchors the mount doesn't approve, or putting one side in a stud and the
  other in anchors
- Trying to lift the TV onto the mount alone
