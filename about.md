Some guys had a dad in the garage handing them a wrench and explaining what they
were doing. A lot of us didn't.

Manskills DIY exists to fill that gap. No lectures, no judgment, just clear
instructions for the everyday maintenance and repair jobs that save you money
and make you self-reliant:

- **Car maintenance**: the basics every driver should be able to handle
- **Home repair**: fixes for the things that wear out and break around the house
- **Small fixes**: mechanical odds and ends, from lawn equipment to loose hinges

Every guide aims to tell you what tools you need, what to do in what order, and
what can hurt you if you're careless. When a job needs specific numbers (fluid
capacities, torque specs, part numbers), always double-check them against your
owner's manual or a factory service manual for your exact model.

If a job is beyond your comfort level or involves serious safety risk, there's
no shame in calling a pro.
