# PHOTO-LOG: Manskills DIY guide images

**Totals:** 51 licensed images downloaded across 11 guides (11 heroes + 40 step images); 48 shot-list entries for original photos.

Compiled 2026-10-09 (America/New_York). Every license below was read on the actual file page (Wikimedia Commons file description page via the Commons API `extmetadata` plus a direct fetch of the page HTML; Flickr photo pages fetched directly and the CC license link read from the page). Search snippets were not relied on.

**How images are stored:** `assets/images/<guide-slug>/`, JPEG, longest side 1600 px or less (most 100–450 KB). Total for 51 files is about 9 MB. Originals were not modified except for resizing/re-encoding (EXIF orientation applied; PNGs flattened to white).

**How images are credited in the drafts:** each image is followed by `<small class="photo-credit">Photo: Author, License, via Source</small>` linking the file page and the license deed. CC BY / CC BY-SA require this; for public-domain and CC0 files it is courtesy only. CC BY-SA applies only to the photo itself (the article text is not affected). If an image is cropped or edited later, add "cropped"/"modified" to its credit.

**Hero:** set as `image:` (path without baseurl; templates and jekyll-seo-tag apply `relative_url`/`absolute_url`) plus `image_alt:` in front matter, and shown at the top of the body with `{{ '/assets/...' | relative_url }}`.

**Shot lists** are also embedded in each draft as `{% comment %}SHOT LIST ...{% endcomment %}` on the relevant step, so they never render.

**Sources checked:** Wikimedia Commons (primary), Openverse (aggregator; used to discover Flickr CC photos, each then verified on Flickr), Flickr CC. Unsplash/Pexels were not used: their licenses allow reuse but they are not CC and their search pages block automated fetches; they are a good manual option for future heroes (record the photo page URL and the Unsplash/Pexels License name). US-government pages (NHTSA, EPA, FEMA) were considered but their how-to images are often stock or third-party licensed, so none were used without a clear PD statement on the file.

**Rejected candidates (for the record):** `File:Faucet Covers for Winter 3M Thinsulate ... (49114182431).jpg` (tagged CC0 but it is a retailer product shot, likely license-laundered; not used). `File:Battery-boost-whitby.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0, but it shows the black clamp on the dead battery's negative post, which contradicts our step 7; not used). `File:Wall mount 9.jpg` (too blurry). `File:Framing (5731781292).jpg` (steel studs; our guide covers wood studs).
