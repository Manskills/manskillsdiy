**Nobody taught me this.** Maybe nobody taught you either.

Manskills DIY is a collection of plain, step-by-step guides for the stuff you're
"supposed to just know": changing your own oil, fixing a running toilet, patching
drywall, replacing a worn belt. Each guide lists the tools you need, walks through
the job one step at a time, and calls out the safety stuff that matters.

Guides are organized into three areas:

- **Car maintenance**: oil changes, filters, brakes, batteries, basic checks
- **Home repair**: plumbing, drywall, doors, fixtures, the stuff that breaks
- **Small fixes**: mowers, small engines, tools, and other mechanical odds and ends
